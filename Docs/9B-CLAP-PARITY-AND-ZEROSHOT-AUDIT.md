## Fresh retrospective audit of previous 8M zero-shot CSV (NOT current 9B bank verification)

Inputs: saved `Atlas_Neural_Bakeoff_04_FIXED/clap_subtype_scores.csv`, the previous 84-file user review CSV, and old `ScannedDatabaseCache_v4.xml`. Fixed eligibility >=2s; no trained classifier or tuned threshold, compare **top-1 text prompt family** to user labels.

- 84 manual records: 12 unavailable in the cached scores, 13 shorter than 2s; **59** eligible matches.
- Of 50 definite percussion yes/no: **7/9** percussion examples were top-1 `Drums`; **1/41** legitimate nonpercussion samples was top-1 `Drums` (the bass `140_001.wav`). **9** additional records were percussion-unsure.
- Of 39 definite non-percussion source-labelled examples: **31/39 (79.5%)** had correct top-1 *source-family agreement*, counting either Strings or Winds under the model's combined **Strings** output. Therefore this is **not** proof of an independent Winds category.
- 8 source misses included guitar loops predicted Rhodes, Keys predicted guitar, and FX predicted vocal. These cases must be retained as hard negatives.
- The previous text prompts may have been adapted to the user's review data. Performance is **not a blind held-out accuracy estimate**, and the previous CSV has not yet been cryptographically compared to the new bank generated on the Mac. Deployment is blocked on validating the actual bank and independent sample packs.
- Relative to the **exploratory** 9A logistic baseline (6/9 drum, 4/41 wrongly suppressed), text prototypes are a promising candidate, NOT a proven upgrade for production.

## Development decision

1. **No installed-plugin change on 9B.** Existing audio decoder, map, sampler, and user overrides remain untouched.
2. Obtain the `clap_text_bank.json` and `clap_subtype_scores.csv` generated by the successful Mac test. Verify text-bank SHA-256 against the reported `summary.json` and compare its scores to the prior CSV.
3. Re-check parity on a larger **stratified** sample (short/long, 44.1/48 kHz, mono/stereo, bass/FX/loop), and investigate the outlier. Avoid building an untrustworthy classifier.
4. After verification: integrate **static text CLAP vectors** compiled into the plugin or loaded as a tiny verified resource. No second neural runtime: keep the existing CLAP AUDIO encoder. Compute normalized 512D audio-vs-text cosine and aggregate prompt groups. Avoid loading HF/transformers at plugin runtime.
5. Calibrate independent percussion and source-family decisions with fixed held-out sample-pack groups; measure both coverage and false inclusion/hiding. Zero-shot raw cosine is NOT a probability. Keep `Unknown` and allow multiple source families only when warranted.
6. Update OneShot/Loop and 2D musical-register coordinates separately using listening-grounded tests. Do not mix shading/colour changes into this step.

No model, runtime code, or filter thresholds are modified in this commit.
